Our Story

Passed down through generations, our resort was founded on a simple promise to protect and honor the majestic California North Coast. For decades, our family has stood as stewards of these cliffs and waters, ensuring that the magic we experienced as children remains unchanged for our guests. Pacific Trails Resort is the culmination of that lifelong devotion, built for those who come seeking beauty, untamed wildlife, quiet adventure, and coastal peace.

Our Values

Grounded in our history, we honor our founders by staying true to our original, humble beginnings and treating every guest like family. We vow to protect this magnificent coastline, keeping it as raw, natural, and wild as it was when we first saw it. Through scenic hiking and calm kayaking, we invite you to explore this landscape deeply, safely, and respectfully as you build your own generational traditions.

We look forward to seeing you!
